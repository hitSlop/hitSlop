# pop 2.0.0

## Mac app

### Important

- pop 2.0.0 is a fresh start. Documents from earlier versions of pop and hitSlop can't be opened by this release: they are never changed, but they are refused with "made by an older version of pop". Create new documents from the templates.
- Sync moves to your pop account. The iCloud Synced Library from earlier versions does not carry over: documents you kept there stay in iCloud and are not imported, and cloud documents and live links made by pop 1.x no longer work. Nothing is converted automatically.
- Need your earlier documents? [pop 1.4.1](https://github.com/hitSlop/dotpop/releases/tag/pop-v1.4.1) still opens them, and 2.0.0 never changes them.

### New

- Sync a document across your devices by signing in. Turn sync on when you create a document or later, without changing the document.
- Share a live link so other people can edit with you in the Mac app or the iPhone app. Joining requires Sign in with Apple. A live link carries the document's saved history, including content you have since removed from view. **Share a Copy** sends a copy without it.

### Improved

- Shared documents that grow too large to keep syncing pause with a clear message and offer **Save Unshared Copy…**. Your edits keep saving locally.
- Sync status uses neutral cloud icons.

## iPhone app

### New

- The same sync and live sharing as the Mac app.
- Opening a pop zooms out of its tile, and swiping back from the left edge returns to your library straight away while the document finishes saving. A document that can't be saved stays open with a Retry on its tile.

## Authoring

- `@hitslop/pop` 2.0.0 opens only pop 2 documents. Older files are refused at open and left unchanged.
