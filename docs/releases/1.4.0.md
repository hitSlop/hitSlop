# pop 1.4.0

## Mac app

### New

- Personalize Pocket Pod with an uploaded background photo and stickers, add local audio files alongside YouTube videos, and play Brick Breaker using the click wheel or keyboard.
- Join the community from the Discord link in Settings.

### Improved

- Updated the catalog header with the dotpop wordmark.
- Pocket Pod now continues importing songs when an individual file fails and keeps unsaved Brick Breaker best scores available to retry.
- Sticker selection follows keyboard focus in Pocket Pod.

## iPhone app

### New

- Create Pocket Pod from Templates, customize its background and stickers, add local songs or YouTube videos, and play Brick Breaker with the wheel or touch controls.
- Join the community from the Discord link in Settings.

### Improved

- Pocket Pod fills the screen edge to edge and keeps YouTube playback inside its display with playback controls on the click wheel.
- Use the iPhone's side buttons for YouTube volume and the wheel to scrub through a track.
- Updated the catalog header with the dotpop wordmark.
