# tinys 1.0.0

## Mac app

- Create personal documents from the included templates, from planners and checklists to notebooks, budgets, and small creative tools.
- Keep each document and its mini app together in a local `.tiny` file. Work offline, save your changes, and reopen where you left off.
- Browse templates in the catalog and return to your recent documents. Create working copies while keeping the original templates intact.
- Export documents as PNG images or PDFs, and preview `.tiny` files in Finder with Quick Look.

## iPhone app

- Open, edit and create tinys on iPhone through TestFlight, including Files import and Home Screen widgets.
- Keep managed documents in sync between Mac and iPhone through your iCloud account.

## Authoring

- Build your own mini apps with TypeScript, Svelte, and the `@hitslop/tinys` SDK, including native window shapes, themes, and custom export views.
- Preview your app while developing, then build a self-contained `.tiny` file.
- Inspect and edit documents from the CLI, or expose named commands for agents and scripts to call.

tinys uses `.tiny` documents and the `tiny` CLI. Legacy hitSlop `.slop` documents and authoring APIs are unsupported. The app preserves the existing Apple account and cloud identities.
