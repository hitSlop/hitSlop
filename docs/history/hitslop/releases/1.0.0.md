# hitSlop 1.0.0

## Mac app

- Create personal documents from the included templates, from planners and checklists to notebooks, budgets, and small creative tools.
- Keep each document and its mini app together in a local `.slop` file. Work offline, save your changes, and reopen where you left off.
- Browse templates in the catalog and return to your recent documents. Create working copies while keeping the original templates intact.
- Export documents as PNG images or PDFs, and preview `.slop` files in Finder with Quick Look.

## Authoring

- Build your own mini apps with TypeScript, Svelte, and the `hitslop` SDK, including native window shapes, themes, and custom export views.
- Preview your app while developing, then build a self-contained `.slop` file.
- Inspect and edit documents from the CLI, or expose named commands for agents and scripts to call.
