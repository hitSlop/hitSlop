# hitSlop 1.3.1

## Mac app

### Fixed

- Pending iCloud deletions can finish syncing when the cloud record has disappeared, instead of repeatedly failing.
- Synced slops that need a newer hitSlop now show update guidance instead of asking you to retry.

### Improved

- Click “iCloud needs attention” for a clear explanation, Retry, Copy diagnostics, and help on Discord.

## iPhone app

### Fixed

- Pending iCloud deletions recover automatically when their cloud record is missing.
- Synced slops that need a newer hitSlop show update guidance.

### Improved

- Sync errors appear in a tappable “iCloud needs attention” status instead of interrupting with an alert.
- The sync help sheet includes Retry, Copy diagnostics, and help on Discord, and is available from Recents and Settings.

The iPhone app is available through [TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/F4qfCkgg). Its build is distributed separately from the Mac and CLI release.
