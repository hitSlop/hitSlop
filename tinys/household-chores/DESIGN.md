# Household Chores design

One job: see whose turn it is and whether the load is fair.

The object is a fridge door: pale enamel with a soft sheen and a brushed edge.
People are glossy round magnets with initials, in six named tones. Chores are
paper notes held by a small magnet, each with a points badge and a day chip. A
two-ring "chore wheel" shows each person's share of the week: the pale outer
ring is points assigned, the solid inner ring points done, with the done count
in the centre. A tally under the wheel carries all-time scores. "New week"
clears done marks, rotates chores marked as rotating to the next person and
bumps the week counter; one change, one undo step.

Text is rounded system sans with a marker face for the title. Bits UI owns the
assignee and day selects. Every state is also a word or mark: done notes are
struck through and checked. The export shows the wheel, scores and notes
without controls. The icon is a wheel with three magnets.
