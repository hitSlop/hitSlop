# Doodle Board

One job: make a quick drawing on a cheerful desktop whiteboard.

The coral frame, warm cream surface, and chunky marker tray belong to this object.
Avenir Next labels and a Georgia empty-state invitation keep it friendly without
making controls cryptic. Selected markers rise slightly; reduced motion removes
transitions. The native window remains the outer boundary.

Start at 744 × 594. Landscape (1000 × 700), square (1000 × 1000), and portrait
(700 × 1000) boards fit the available stage proportionally. In the Mac app, opening
and choosing a preset fit the window around a 720px drawing long edge plus measured
chrome, bounded by the display. Manual resizing stays free; Fit board restores the
fit. Presets clip from the top-left without changing existing stroke geometry.

The bottom Tools tab reveals an overlay on hover, click, or T. Pin keeps it open;
otherwise hover, focus, and child popovers hold it open, with a 300ms exit delay.
Crossing the tab during drawing never opens it. Controls wrap at narrow widths,
and the tray/popovers scroll at small heights. P/E select pen and eraser; Escape
closes a child popover before the unpinned tray. Drawing requires a pointer.

Five marker colors, three width shortcuts, and a Brush popover make freehand strokes.
Brush settings offer size 1–64, smoothing, pressure variation, and symmetric taper.
A sample stroke previews changes. Brush settings and pinning reset when reopened;
each gesture snapshots its settings, and finished ink never changes. Erasing removes whole
strokes, topmost first. Clear confirms removal of all ink, including hidden overflow.
Colors are resolved when drawn so later theme changes do not recolor saved artwork.

Exports contain the selected board and ink only, including an empty cream board
when blank. The icon is a coral board with a blue doodle and three colorful markers.
No undo, pan/zoom, custom dimensions, or shape editing is included.
