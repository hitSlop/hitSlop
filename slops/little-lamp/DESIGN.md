# Little Lamp

The object is the interface: one warm enamel lamp floating on the desktop. No
title, caption, panel or separate control. The 320×320 transparent window frames
the full reach of the head, jointed arm and softly lit pool.

A butter-yellow shade, copper edges, brass links and a weighted oval foot make
the character readable over light and dark desktops. Two small eyes sit in the
enamel; sleepy lids and a tilted head communicate off, while open eyes and light
communicate on. The accessible tap target exposes the saved pressed state and
supports keyboard activation. A warm outline appears only for keyboard focus.

Rive owns the performance: a two-bone IK chain reaches a head target, while
coordinated timeline keys lift, squash and tilt the shade, open the eyes and flicker
the light. Pointer offsets move the target and tilt the head. An awake loop adds
gentle breathing and a double blink. Sleep ends in a compact slouched pose.

The four states can interrupt each other without waiting for a clip to finish.
A fresh open, reduced motion and captures go directly to the saved pose. Captures
reuse the real renderer with idle playback stopped; all backgrounds stay transparent.

The native mask covers the motion envelope, so corners are outside the window.
It cannot follow each animated pixel. The small focus accent is themeable;
the illustrated character's palette belongs to the Rive artwork.
