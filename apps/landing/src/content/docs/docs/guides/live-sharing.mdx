---
title: Live sharing (preview)
description: Share an editable link, join on Mac, iPhone or Chrome, and keep your copy.
---

Live collaboration is available in the **developer/TestFlight preview**. It is not
included in ordinary Mac releases. The iPhone app is in TestFlight for iOS 26+;
its App Store release is coming soon. See [availability](/docs/availability/).

## Share a live link

Open a slop on Mac and turn on the **Live collaboration** toggle in the **Share**
popover. On iPhone, open the document actions menu and choose **Collaborate**.
The owner signs in with Apple to create the link, then chooses **Copy Link**.
Send it to the people you want to edit with.
**Anyone with the link can edit**, so pass it on with care. Guests need no account.

Universal links open hitSlop when it is installed and enabled for sharing. Otherwise,
open the link in Chrome to use the browser copy. Other browsers remain unqualified.

Open the link in the hitSlop app on Mac or iPhone, or use Chrome at
[share.hitslop.com](https://share.hitslop.com). Each person edits their own copy;
changes flow between connected copies. Edits made while a copy is open offline
merge when it reconnects.

## Keep a browser copy

Chrome saves your copy in that browser on that device. Opening a shared slop in the
browser requires a connection; reliable offline reopening remains future work.
Download a `.slop` file to keep a copy you can open in hitSlop. Browser storage can
be cleared or evicted, so download work you want to keep.

## Stop sharing

The owner turns off **Live collaboration**, then confirms **Stop**. The confirmation
explains that the link stops working and everyone keeps their own copy. Those copies
remain editable independently. Sharing again creates a new link; the old link stays ended.

Open the new link to rejoin. A matching local document keeps its identity and current
edits, then merges with the new share. hitSlop must confirm that the previous link ended
before replacing its membership; if that check fails, retry when connected.

## Save an independent copy

Choose **Save Unshared Copy…** while collaboration is live to keep an independent
file with a new document identity and no live link. It keeps accepted edits and
referenced attachments, without the shared history. It does not stop the original link.

## Delete your sharing account

In the account panel, choose **Delete Account…** and confirm. Deletion ends your live
links and removes their relay data and your sharing account. Documents on your devices
and in iCloud stay. The app also attempts to revoke Apple access; if that cannot finish,
it directs you to remove hitSlop in Apple Account settings. Guests' downloaded and local
copies remain theirs. Signing out alone does not end existing links.

For an independent file, image or PDF instead, see
[build and share](/docs/guides/build-and-share/). For your own Mac and iPhone,
[Synced Library](/docs/availability/) uses your iCloud account separately from live links.
