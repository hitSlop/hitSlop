---
title: Live sharing
description: Share an editable link, join on Mac, iPhone or Chrome, and keep your copy.
---

Live collaboration is available in the Mac app and the iPhone app on TestFlight. The iPhone app is in TestFlight for iOS 26+;
its App Store release is coming soon. See [availability](/docs/availability/).

## Share a live link

Open a pop on Mac and turn on the **Live collaboration** toggle in the **Share**
popover. On iPhone, open the document actions menu and choose **Collaborate**.
The owner signs in with Apple to create the link, then chooses **Copy Link**.
Send it to the people you want to edit with.
**Anyone with the link can edit**, so pass it on with care. Guests need no account.

The link opens pop on Mac or iPhone when it is installed. Otherwise, open it in
Chrome at [share.dotpop.click](https://share.dotpop.click) to use the browser copy. Other
browsers remain unqualified. Each person edits their own copy; changes flow between
connected copies. Edits made while a copy is open offline merge when it reconnects.

Live sharing includes retained history, including content removed from view. Routine
compaction does not erase it. Use **Share a Copy** for an independent, sanitized copy
when you want to send only the accepted visible state and referenced attachments.

A pop whose app and saved history together exceed 16 MiB cannot be shared. Imported
files such as photos do not count: they transfer separately under their own limits, so
keep large media in them. pop checks this before creating the shared document and keeps
your local file.

Synced documents remain in your cloud library until you delete them.

## Keep a browser copy

Chrome saves your copy in that browser on that device. Opening a shared pop in the
browser requires a connection; reliable offline reopening remains future work.
Download a `.pop` file to keep a copy you can open in pop. Browser storage can
be cleared or evicted, so download work you want to keep.

## Stop sharing

The owner turns off **Live collaboration**, then confirms **Stop**. The confirmation
explains that the link stops working and everyone keeps their own copy. Those copies
remain editable independently. Sharing again creates a new link; the old link stays ended.

Open the new link to rejoin. A matching local document keeps its identity and current
edits, then merges with the new share. Access is checked against the current invitation; an old invitation cannot reconnect.

## Save an independent copy

Choose **Save Unshared Copy…** while collaboration is live to keep an independent
file with a new document identity and no live link. It keeps accepted edits and
referenced attachments, without the shared history. It does not stop the original link.

## Delete your sharing account

In the account panel, choose **Delete Account…** and confirm. Deletion revokes your live
links, deletes your owned cloud documents, removes joined memberships and deletes your
account. Downloaded local copies remain on devices. The app also attempts to revoke Apple access; if that cannot finish,
it directs you to remove pop in Apple Account settings. Guests' downloaded and local
copies remain theirs. Signing out alone does not end existing links.

For an independent file, image or PDF instead, see
[build and share](/docs/guides/build-and-share/). For your own Mac and iPhone,
[Synced Library](/docs/availability/) discovers documents through your account and
syncs them through the same cloud document service. Stopping collaboration keeps your
own devices syncing.
