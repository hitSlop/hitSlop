---
title: Availability
---

hitSlop is available for macOS 15.2+ on Apple silicon, with bundled and local
templates, Recents, document editing, imported file attachments, and PNG/PDF export.
Creating, previewing and building slops, rendering template artwork, editing documents,
and exporting closed documents as PNG/PDF also work on Linux with Bun.

The **iPhone app** for **iOS 26+** is in **TestFlight preview**; the App Store release
is coming soon. It opens, edits and creates slops, with Quick Checklist as its starter,
and imports `.slop` files from Files.

The preview's **Synced Library** syncs managed slops through your private iCloud
storage, transferring whole files and merging document edits. Sign in to the same
iCloud account on your devices; no separate sharing account is needed for library sync.
Debug builds use CloudKit Development; TestFlight and App Store builds use Production.
These are separate environments. The ordinary Mac release keeps the library off until
its Production rollout, so a Debug Mac and a TestFlight iPhone do not share a library.

If iCloud is unavailable, syncing stops. On an account switch, the previous account's
local slops stay on the device and stop syncing; they are not uploaded to the new account.
This managed library is separate from editing active `.slop` files in iCloud Drive or
another synced folder, which remains unsupported. On Mac, a document in iCloud offers
**Move to ~/hitSlop and Open** to move it into local storage before editing.

macOS desktop widgets display saved document images in small, medium and large sizes
when the embedded app includes a widget view. The iPhone preview adds Home Screen
widgets for library slops with a widget view, rendered on the phone; tap to open the
slop. iPhone widgets also refresh in the background when iOS allows. See
[widget setup and refresh behavior](/docs/guides/icons-and-exports/#design-a-macos-widget).

**Live collaboration is a developer/TestFlight preview**, absent from ordinary Mac
releases. Share a live link so anyone with it can edit in hitSlop on Mac or iPhone,
or in Chrome at share.hitslop.com. See [live sharing](/docs/guides/live-sharing/)
for sign-in, browser copies and stopping a link.

The separate local Chrome browser beta opens independent document copies with local
browser storage and downloads a complete `.slop` file through Save As. It does not
connect to the original Mac document. Safari and mobile browsers remain unqualified.

On Mac, apps may declare camera and microphone capture. The iPhone preview refuses
camera and microphone requests and does not provide speech transcription. Native on-device speech recognition
transcribes saved recordings when the Mac supports the language and speech permission
is granted. It is not available in `slop dev` or browser copies; see
[capabilities and permissions](/docs/guides/capabilities/) for the support matrix.

Future work includes hosted catalog and template publishing, other native platforms,
a document history view, and app/schema upgrades. Windows, Android and Linux native
apps are longer-term directions without release dates. Per-slop permission decisions
tied to an app's code and additional device capabilities remain proposals.

Pre-launch development documents are unsupported and are not migrated. From the first
public release, newer hitSlop builds must keep released documents openable. Installing
a newer host never automatically replaces the app embedded in an existing document.
