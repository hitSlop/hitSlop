---
title: Availability
---

Available: macOS 15.2+ on Apple silicon, bundled and local templates, recents, native document editing, and PNG/PDF export. Creating, previewing and building slops also works on Linux with Bun.

Coming soon: [live collaboration](/docs/guides/live-sharing/). The website preview uses simulated participants.

Future work: hosted catalog, hosted template publishing, media import, iCloud and other synced folders, iOS, Windows, a document history view, and schema evolution. Documents from earlier builds are not supported and are not migrated.
