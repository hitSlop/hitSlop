---
title: Availability
---

pop is available for macOS 15.2+ on Apple silicon, with bundled and local
templates, Recents, document editing, imported file attachments, and PNG/PDF export.
Creating, previewing and building pop, rendering template artwork, editing documents,
and exporting closed documents as PNG/PDF also work on Linux with Bun.

## Get the iPhone app

The **iPhone app** for **iOS 26+** is in **TestFlight**; the App Store release
is coming soon. [Install it through TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/F4qfCkgg) on your iPhone. The app opens, edits and creates pop, with the Popular templates, Little Days among them, as starters,
and imports `.pop` files from Files.

## Library, widgets and collaboration

The **Synced Library** discovers documents through Firestore and synchronizes their
contents through Cloudflare. Sign in to the same pop account on Mac and iPhone.

Creation offers **This device** and **Sync across devices**. You can enable sync later
without replacing the document or changing its identity. A second device shows the
library and downloads complete documents when opened; downloaded copies work offline.

Signing out hides account documents and pauses sync while retaining downloaded files
and pending edits. Independent local copies remain available. Existing downloaded
CloudKit files remain local; cloud-only files need download/export with the previous app
before the clean cutover. The new app does not delete old CloudKit records.

Keep active standalone `.pop` files outside iCloud Drive and other file-sync folders.
On Mac, a document there offers **Move to ~/pop and Open** before editing.

macOS desktop widgets display saved document images in small, medium and large sizes
when the embedded app includes a widget view. The iPhone app adds Home Screen
widgets for library pop with a widget view, rendered on the phone; tap to open the
pop. iPhone widgets also refresh in the background when iOS allows. See
[widget setup and refresh behavior](/docs/guides/icons-and-exports/#design-a-widget).

**Live collaboration** is available in the Mac app and the iPhone app on TestFlight. Share a live link so anyone with it can edit in pop on Mac or iPhone,
or in Chrome at share.dotpop.click. See [live sharing](/docs/guides/live-sharing/)
for sign-in, browser copies and stopping a link.

Local browser copies, opened with `pop open --browser`, are in beta. They open
independent document copies in Chrome on desktop with local browser storage and
download a complete `.pop` file through Save As. A copy does not connect to the
original Mac document. Safari and mobile browsers remain unqualified.

Camera, microphone and speech recognition depend on the host. See
[capabilities and permissions](/docs/guides/capabilities/) for the support matrix.

Future work includes hosted catalog and template publishing, other native platforms,
a document history view, and app/schema upgrades. Windows, Android and Linux native
apps are longer-term directions without release dates. Per-pop permission decisions
tied to an app's code and additional device capabilities remain proposals.

Newer pop opens documents from every released version. Installing
a newer host never automatically replaces the app embedded in an existing document.
