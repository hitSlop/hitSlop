# Tiny Planet design

One job: a small world you enjoy turning around and slowly filling in.

The object is a faceted planet floating in a violet night: ocean, sand, grass, rock
and snow, coloured per face by height and by season. Tools sit in a frosted bar at
the foot (tree, house, rock, crystal, remove); the four seasons and a dice for a new
world stack at the right edge. Tapping land places the selected thing upright on the
surface; dragging orbits; pinch or scroll zooms. Open water refuses, in words.

Saved: name, seed, season, and each thing's kind, latitude and longitude. The terrain
is a pure function of the seed, so a reopened document rebuilds the same world, and
rolling a new one re-seats everything on it. Camera, tool and pop-in are presentation.
Slow auto-spin pauses while you drag and stops for reduced motion; things appear
without the grow animation. Rendering pauses when the window is hidden. Export shows
a still of the planet with its name and count; the icon is a flat planet with a tree,
a house and a crystal.
