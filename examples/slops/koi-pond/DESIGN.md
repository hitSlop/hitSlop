# Koi Pond

One job: keep a few cute koi company on your desktop.

A round, transparent 420 × 420 window holds a watercolor pond: stone rim, moss, weeds, depth
glazes, and lily pads with lotus blossoms that drift slowly above the fish. The koi are drawn
with soft outlines, dot eyes and rosy cheeks, and they cast shadows on the floor.

Hovering reveals a paper pill of controls (Feed, Add koi, Your koi, Repaint) and a small badge
showing the feed count. Clicking the water drops pellets and the nearest koi swims over to eat.
Hovering a fish shows its name. Keys: F feed, K add, R repaint. There are at most nine koi. While a new pond paints, the brush
button wobbles, the old pond stays live, and the new one fades in when ready.

With reduced motion, the pond holds still: no swimming, ripples or drift, and feeding just
reports which koi ate. Exports show a still of the pond with names and the feed count. The icon
is two curled koi and a lily pad in a round pond.
