# Object families

These directions illustrate how purpose can shape appearance. Borrow useful
relationships between content and form; choose fresh colors, proportions, and
details for the actual task. No reference images or particular retro style are
required.

## Paper

Use for an artifact people read, edit, share, print, or export: invoice, recipe,
résumé, planner, notes. Favor editorial hierarchy, direct editing, generous
reading rhythm, fine rules, and a flat export state. Editing affordances can be
quiet, but focus and selection must remain obvious.

An itinerary might borrow boarding-pass structure: a prominent origin and
destination, aligned departure details, and a fine perforation-like divider
before the daily schedule. A reading list could use library-card typography,
ruled rows, and small date stamps that show actual reading dates. A recipe may
use warm paper, a confident editorial title, and a clear ingredients-to-method
rhythm. Let real content supply the detail; omit invented barcodes and stamps.

Avoid placing the paper inside another rounded card. Avoid permanent toolbars in
the export. Do not use fixed-height nested scrollers for printable content.

## Instrument

Use for a compact control surface: timer, tracker, picker, mixer, calculator.
Prioritize one live readout or action, group related controls like hardware,
make modes/state unmistakable, and use restrained depth only to communicate
operation.

A budget tool might group a strong balance readout, aligned ledger amounts,
and calculator-like controls for entering an expense. A timer can use a bold
numeric face, a recessed display, and one unmistakable start/pause control.
An ambient mixer can use slender faders and small level indicators tied to
actual values. Materials may be dark glass, bright plastic, or clean flat CSS;
controls should map to real functions, with precise values always readable.

Avoid dashboard grids, decorative gauges, excessive status pills, and many
equal-weight actions.

## Skin

Use when silhouette is essential to recognition or delight: radio, pocket
device, novelty utility. The exact-size RGBA PNG is both backing and native
hit-test mask. Keep controls inside the solid safe area and verify pointer
behavior around holes and feathered alpha.

A habit tracker might feel like a cheerful pocket device, with a rounded body,
a legible weekly grid, and a satisfying completion control. A countdown could
use a compact clock silhouette around one dominant readout. Keep the shell
subordinate to the task: no decorative buttons that appear operable, and no
loss of usable space just to imitate hardware.

Avoid a skin when rounded/capsule CSS already communicates the object. A skin is
fixed-size and trades flexibility for a meaningful silhouette.

## Hybrids

Choose one dominant family and borrow one supporting behavior. A recipe may be
Paper with an Instrument timer; an invoice may adopt a receipt Skin. The job,
not visual novelty, decides.
